Dolphin House Swimming & Snorkeling Adventure

⭐ 4.9 (218 Reviews) ⏱️ 4 Hours (Morning Cruise) 📍 Northern Coral Reefs 🤿 Snorkel Gear & Snacks Included

Trip Overview

Experience an ethical encounter with wild spinner dolphins in their natural open-sea sanctuary at Dolphin House Reef. Depart early in the morning when the dolphins are most active and playful. Swim alongside these magnificent marine mammals under the guidance of certified rescue instructors, followed by a snorkeling session at an untouched coral garden.

Daily Schedule & Itinerary

07:00 AM – 07:30 AM

Early Morning Hotel Pickup

Prompt transfer from your hotel or accommodation direct to the departure pier.

08:00 AM

Speed Cruise to Dolphin Habitat

Sail toward the protected northern reefs where wild pods congregate daily.

08:45 AM – 10:15 AM

Dolphin Watching & Swimming

Observe and swim with dolphins in crystal-clear waters with guide supervision.

10:45 AM

Coral Reef Snorkeling Session

Snorkel around colorful coral pinnacles with clownfish and sea turtles.

12:00 PM

Arrival & Return Hotel Transfer

Fresh fruit snacks and cold drinks on board before returning to your hotel by noon.
