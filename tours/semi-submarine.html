Semi-Submarine Underwater Observatory Tour

⭐ 4.8 (152 Reviews) ⏱️ 2 Hours 📍 Hurghada Coastline 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Ideal for Families & Kids

Trip Overview

The ultimate marine experience for families with toddlers, elders, and non-swimmers! Step inside an air-conditioned underwater observation salon submerged 3 meters beneath the waves. Through wide panoramic windows, enjoy an unobstructed cinema view of the Red Sea's coral gardens and tropical marine life without getting wet.
