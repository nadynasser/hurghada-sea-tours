Orange Bay Island & Snorkeling Boat Cruise

⭐ 4.8 (324 Reviews) ⏱️ 7 Hours (Full Day) 📍 Giftun Island 🍽️ Buffet Lunch Included

Trip Overview

Escape to the Caribbean of Egypt with a full-day luxury cruise to Orange Bay on Giftun Island. Renowned for its powdery white sands, rustic wooden walkways, and shallow crystal-clear lagoons, Orange Bay is the premier island destination in Hurghada. Enjoy two guided snorkeling stops at vibrant living coral reefs teeming with exotic marine life, followed by an open seafood buffet on the yacht's sun deck.

Daily Schedule & Itinerary

08:00 AM – 08:30 AM

Hotel Pickup

Air-conditioned transfer from your hotel lobby directly to the cruise departure pier.

09:00 AM

Scenic Cruise Direct to Island

Board our luxury yacht, enjoy welcome drinks, and sail across the turquoise Red Sea waters.

09:45 AM – 11:45 AM

Orange Bay Island Visit (First Stop)

Disembark onto the island first! Enjoy 2 full hours relaxing on powdery white sand, photo sessions on water swings, and swimming in shallow crystal waters.

12:15 PM

1st Snorkeling Stop: Living Coral Reef

Guided snorkeling session with provided sanitized masks, fins, and life jackets to spot clownfish, rays, and vibrant corals.

01:15 PM

Open Buffet Lunch on Board

Freshly prepared hot buffet featuring grilled seafood or chicken, pasta, fresh salads, seasonal fruits, and soft drinks.

02:15 PM

2nd Guided Snorkeling Stop

Second snorkeling stop at a protected reef sanctuary to explore colorful coral pinnacles and exotic marine life.

04:00 PM – 04:30 PM

Cruise Return & Hotel Drop-Off

Relax on the sun deck during the cruise back to the pier, followed by comfortable transfer back to your hotel.
