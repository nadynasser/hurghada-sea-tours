Living Coral Reef & Marine Sanctuary Excursion

⭐ 4.9 (184 Reviews) ⏱️ 5 Hours 📍 Giftun Marine National Park 🍽️ Hot Buffet Lunch Included

Trip Overview

Immerse yourself in one of the richest marine ecosystems in the world. This excursion targets two renowned offshore coral reef plateaus far from crowded shores. Discover hundreds of vibrant fish species, manta rays, and sea turtles accompanied by certified marine life specialists.

Daily Schedule & Itinerary

08:30 AM

Hotel Pickup

Transfer from your hotel to the cruise pier.

09:15 AM

Departure to Marine Sanctuary

Sailing past the coastal islands into the protected coral marine park.

10:30 AM

1st Snorkel Stop: Coral Gardens

Guided 1-hour drift snorkel along shallow, protected coral pinnacles.

12:15 PM

Yacht Buffet Lunch

Hot grilled lunch served on deck with fresh salads and cold drinks.

01:15 PM

2nd Snorkel Stop: Deep Drop-Off

Explore deep reef walls with higher chances of seeing sea turtles and rays.

02:30 PM

Return to Marina & Drop-Off

Relax on the sun deck on the journey back to Hurghada.
