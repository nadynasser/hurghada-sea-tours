Hula Hula Island & Snorkeling Boat Cruise

⭐ 4.9 (218 Reviews) ⏱️ 7.5 Hours (Full Day) 📍 Giftun Island 🍽️ Buffet Lunch & Watersports

Trip Overview

Immerse yourself in the tropical Hawaiian vibes of the Red Sea on the all-new Hula Hula Island cruise! Located on the pristine shores of Giftun Island, Hula Hula boasts powdery white beaches, charming tiki huts, picturesque wooden swings placed directly in the crystal-clear shallow lagoon, and relaxing shoulder massages right on the shore. This unforgettable trip includes two guided coral reef snorkeling stops, fun banana boat and sofa water rides, plus a delicious freshly cooked buffet lunch served on our luxury cruiser.

Daily Schedule & Itinerary

08:00 AM – 08:30 AM

Hotel Pickup

Comfortable round-trip transfer from your Hurghada hotel directly to the departure boat pier.

09:00 AM

Departure & Cruising Begins

Board our spacious two-deck cruiser and sail into the open azure Red Sea.

10:30 AM

1st Snorkeling Session

Explore pristine coral reefs teeming with colorful exotic fish alongside certified snorkel guides.

12:00 PM – 02:00 PM

2 Hours on Hula Hula Island

Disembark on the tropical sandy beach. Enjoy the wooden water swings, sun loungers, crystal swimming, and serene island ambiance.

02:15 PM

Open Buffet Lunch on Board

Enjoy a rich hot buffet with grilled fish, chicken, pasta, rice, fresh salads, seasonal fruits, and free soft drinks.

03:00 PM

2nd Snorkeling Stop & Watersports

Second reef exploration plus thrilling banana boat and crazy sofa rides behind the speed vessel.

04:30 PM

Cruise Return & Hotel Transfer

Sail back to the pier and enjoy air-conditioned transfer back to your resort.
