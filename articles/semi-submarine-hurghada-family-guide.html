Semi-Submarine Hurghada: Kids & Family Underwater Guide (2026)

✍️ Written by Hurghada Sea Tours Editorial • ⏱️ 5 min read • 📍 Hurghada Marine Sanctuary • 📅 Scheduled: Monday, Nov 30, 2026

The Red Sea is universally celebrated for holding some of the most biodiverse marine ecosystems on Earth. However, for families traveling with toddlers, seniors, or non-swimmers who feel hesitant about jumping into open water with a snorkel mask, exploring these wonders can feel challenging. This is where the semi submarine Hurghada tour shines as the premier family excursion.

A semi-submarine combines the thrill of a submarine ride with the comfort of a luxury boat cruise. Rather than fully submerging like a military submarine, the vessel stays on the surface while its lower deck sits 3 to 4 meters below the water line, featuring massive panoramic glass windows on both sides.

How the Semi-Submarine Works

The vessel features two distinct levels designed for traveler comfort:

  • The Underwater Observatory (Lower Hull): A modern, air-conditioned cabin equipped with individual swivel armchairs facing expansive optical glass windows. Here, you are surrounded on all sides by living coral gardens, clownfish, moray eels, and passing sea turtles.
  • The Open-Air Panoramic Sun Deck (Upper Deck): A spacious shaded deck where travelers enjoy refreshing sea breezes, panoramic views of the Hurghada coastline, and complimentary beverages.

🐠 Diver Photo Show Included

During the underwater observation, our professional scuba diver swims outside the panoramic windows carrying fish treats. Curious schools of angelfish, butterflyfish, and parrotfish gather right in front of the glass, creating incredible photo and video opportunities for kids!

What Marine Life Will You See?

The semi submarine navigates through the protected coastal reef gardens of Hurghada where marine life flourishes in calm, clear water:

  • Brain & Table Corals: Living coral colonies that have grown undisturbed for centuries.
  • Red Sea Clownfish (Nemo): Nestled safely within the stinging tentacles of sea anemones.
  • Blue-Spotted Ribbon-Tail Rays: Gliding gracefully across the sandy sea floor below the observatory.
  • Giant Pufferfish & Lionfish: Frequently spotted hovering around the shaded crevices of the reef wall.

Practical Tips for Families & Kids

  • Departure Times: Trips depart three times daily (9:00 AM, 11:30 AM, and 2:00 PM). The morning 9:00 AM trip typically offers the calmest seas and best sun lighting for photography.
  • Motion Sickness: Because the lower hull is fully stabilized and stays in sheltered inshore waters, seasickness is rare compared to open-sea speedboats.
  • Optional Snorkeling: For family members who wish to swim, the boat anchors for 30 minutes where adults and older kids can jump in from the swim platform with complimentary masks and life vests.

Frequently Asked Questions

How deep does the semi submarine go in Hurghada?
The air-conditioned underwater observation deck is situated between 3 and 4 meters below the surface of the Red Sea, offering eye-level views of coral pinnacles and tropical fish.
Is the semi submarine in Hurghada suitable for toddlers and seniors?
Yes! The semi-submarine is the single best sea excursion for non-swimmers, toddlers, and elderly travelers because you remain completely dry in comfortable swivel seats in an air-conditioned cabin.
How much does a semi submarine ticket cost in Hurghada?
Tickets with Hurghada Sea Tours cost €20 per adult and €10 per child (ages 4-10), while infants under 4 join completely free. The price includes hotel transfers, underwater tour, and an optional 30-minute snorkeling session from the sundeck.
