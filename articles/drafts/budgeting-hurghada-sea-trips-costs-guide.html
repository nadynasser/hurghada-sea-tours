How Much Do Boat Trips Cost in Hurghada? Price Guide (2026)

✍️ Written by Hurghada Sea Tours Finance & Booking Desk • ⏱️ 6 min read • 📍 Hurghada, Egypt • 📅 Scheduled Release: Friday, Dec 11, 2026

If you are planning your vacation budget, you are likely asking: how much do boat trips cost in Hurghada? Because prices range wildly between hotel lobby desks, street hawkers, and online travel agencies (OTAs), many travelers worry about overpaying or falling victim to hidden port charges.

The good news is that Hurghada remains one of the world's most affordable destinations for world-class maritime excursions. In 2026, a full-day guided sea trip with lunch and snorkeling gear costs between €20 and €35 per adult for shared group excursions, while exclusive private yachts start from €150 to €300 per group. Below is a complete, transparent breakdown of expected costs.

Understanding Hidden Fees & National Park Taxes

When comparing offers, always confirm what is included in the stated quote to avoid unexpected surprises:

  • National Marine Park Tax ($5 / €5 per person): Egyptian environmental laws mandate a park fee for entering the Giftun Island National Protected Area. Legitimate operators explicitly mention whether this fee is included or payable at harbor check-in.
  • Far-Hotel Transfer Surcharges: Most standard packages include complimentary hotel pickup within central Hurghada (El Dahar, Sakala, and Village Road). Resorts in Makadi Bay, Sahl Hasheesh, El Gouna, or Soma Bay may have a small shuttle surcharge ($5 to $10 per vehicle).
  • Professional Photos & Video USBs: Freelance camera operators aboard boats charge between €25 and €40 for digital albums. These packages are completely optional.

Shared Boat Excursions vs. Private Yacht Charters: Value Breakdown

Deciding between a shared group boat tour and a private yacht charter depends largely on party size, budget, and desired privacy:

  • Shared Group Excursions (€20 – €35 per person): Unbeatable value for solo travelers, couples, and small families. Modern 3-deck yachts comfortably accommodate 25 to 40 passengers with ample space across shaded lounges and sunbeds. All food, drinks, and guidance are shared.
  • Private Yacht Charters (€180 – €320 total per boat): If traveling as a family or group of 5 to 10 friends, private charters often cost roughly the same per person as shared tours. You gain full control over departure timings, spend longer at uncrowded reefs, choose your music, and enjoy personal chef service.
  • Speedboat Private Rentals (€120 – €180 total): Rapid point-to-point transfers to Giftun Island or dolphin lagoons, ideal for those wanting 2 to 4 hours on the water without spending an entire 8-hour day on a cruising yacht.

Payment Methods & Currency Tips in Hurghada

When settling tour payments and onboard expenses in Egypt:

  1. Cash is Preferred by Local Operators: Cash in Euros, US Dollars, British Pounds, or Egyptian Pounds eliminates international credit card processing fees (typically 3% to 4%).
  2. Bank Notes Condition: Ensure foreign bills (USD/EUR) are clean, untorn, and issued after 2013, as Egyptian banks reject creased or damaged foreign currency.
  3. ATM Withdrawals: ATMs are abundant across Hurghada hotels and marinas, dispensing Egyptian Pounds at official central bank exchange rates.

💰 Why Direct Booking Saves You 30% to 50%

Booking directly with a licensed local operator via WhatsApp avoids the substantial 25%–40% commission markups taken by hotel tour desks and third-party booking aggregator portals.

Tipping Etiquette on Red Sea Boat Cruises

Tipping ("Baksheesh") is customary in Egypt. The yacht crew, dive guides, and onboard cooks work diligently to ensure your safety and enjoyment throughout an 8-hour voyage. A polite tip of 100 to 200 EGP (€2 to €4) per guest placed in the communal tip box at the end of the journey is standard and appreciated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there extra hidden fees when booking a boat tour in Hurghada?
By Egyptian law, an official National Marine Park entrance tax (typically $5 or €5 per person) applies to all marine protected areas around Giftun Island. Reputable direct operators state this clearly upfront.
Is it cheaper to book online directly or through hotel lobby reps?
Booking directly with local licensed operators via direct chat or official websites is typically 30% to 50% cheaper than hotel kiosks, which charge heavy hotel-commission markups.
