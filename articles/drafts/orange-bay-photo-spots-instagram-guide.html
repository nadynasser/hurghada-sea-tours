Orange Bay Hurghada: Best Photo Spots & Instagram Tips (2026)

✍️ Written by Hurghada Sea Tours Media Crew • ⏱️ 5 min read • 📍 Giftun Island National Park • 📅 Scheduled Release: Tuesday, Dec 15, 2026

Known internationally as the "Egyptian Maldives", Giftun Island's premier beach club is a dream for content creators and travel enthusiasts. Master these essential Orange Bay photography tips Hurghada travelers rely on to capture breathtaking, Instagram-ready images of radiant turquoise waters and pristine white sandbars.

Because most cruise boats arrive around the same time between 11:00 AM and 1:00 PM, having a plan for your photoshoot ensures you get clear, crowd-free shots before the beach fills up. Here is our insider guide to the top photo locations and technical tips.

The Top 4 Photo Spots at Orange Bay

1. The In-Water Wooden Swings

Set directly into the waist-deep shallow lagoon, these iconic wooden swings offer the quintessential Maldives-style shot. Pose facing the horizon or looking over your shoulder as gentle turquoise waves lap at your feet.

2. The Long Wooden Pier & Thatched Umbrellas

The elevated wooden pier stretching from the boat dock to the beach provides dramatic leading lines. Capture low-angle portraits with the natural straw umbrellas and gradient blue waters in the background.

3. The High Wooden Castle Observation Deck

Climb to the upper terrace of the rustic wooden restaurant structure for a breathtaking panoramic view of the entire bay. From here, you can photograph the contrasting shades of navy, cyan, and shimmering sandy shoals.

4. The Sunken Wooden Hammocks

Relax in the shallow submerged hammocks located on the quieter western side of the beach bar. It is the ultimate spot for serene, luxury lifestyle portraits.

Lighting, Timing & Camera Settings

To maximize the brilliance of the water color and avoid harsh facial shadows:

  • Shoot Between 11:30 AM and 1:30 PM for Water Color: While golden hour is great for warm portraits, midday sun directly overhead penetrates the crystal-clear water to reveal the vibrant turquoise tones the Red Sea is famous for.
  • Use a Circular Polarizer (CPL Filter): If using a DSLR or mirrorless camera, a CPL filter eliminates water surface reflection, allowing your lens to capture the glowing white sand and marine life underneath.
  • Turn on HDR on Smartphones: The contrast between bright desert sand and shaded wooden structures is high. HDR balances shadow details without blowing out the sky.
  • Wear Contrasting Colors: Bright coral, white linen, red, or tropical yellow swimwear pops dramatically against the blue lagoon backdrop.

Camera Gear & Recommended Smartphone Focal Lengths

Whether you are using the latest iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, or a professional mirrorless setup, smart lens selection transforms standard holiday snapshots into magazine-worthy editorial captures:

  • Ultra-Wide Lens (0.5x / 14-24mm): Perfect for the wooden castle lookouts and expansive panorama shots showing the sweeping contrast between desert mountains, golden sandbars, and the azure sea.
  • Portrait Mode (2x or 3x / 50-85mm): Best for the wooden water swings and beach hammocks. Compressing the background blurs distracting beachgoers and makes you stand out crisply against the turquoise waters.
  • Under-Water Split Shots: By positioning a dome port or waterproof casing halfway submerged at the waterline, you can capture the blue sky and wooden swing above water while capturing white sand and tropical fish below the surface simultaneously.

Best Time of Day & Beating the Crowds

Orange Bay operates on scheduled boat docking waves. To secure clean photos with nobody in your background:

  1. Head Straight to the Water Swings at Arrival: Most tour groups stop at the wooden entrance sign or bathroom facilities upon disembarking. Walk directly to the furthest water swings within the first 10 minutes to enjoy empty backgrounds.
  2. Capture Elevated Architecture During Lunch Hours: Between 12:30 PM and 1:30 PM, beach visitors congregate under shaded restaurant pergolas for refreshments, leaving the long wooden piers and boardwalks open.
  3. Save Lifestyle Hammock Shots for Last: The west-facing hammocks get the best soft lighting as the early afternoon sun starts to angle, softening midday facial shadows.

⚠️ Important Drone Regulations in Egypt

Please note that flying camera drones (such as DJI Mavic or Mini series) is strictly illegal in Egypt without comprehensive military defense clearance. Do not bring drones on boat excursions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I fly a camera drone at Orange Bay Island?
No. Under Egyptian civil aviation laws, recreational drones are strictly prohibited without specialized national defense permits. Border customs will confiscate unpermitted drones at the airport.
What time of day offers the best light for photos at Orange Bay?
Midday between 11:30 AM and 2:00 PM provides the strongest overhead sunlight, making the shallow lagoon water glow in electric turquoise hues.
