What to Bring on a Hurghada Boat Trip: Packing Checklist (2026)

✍️ Written by Hurghada Sea Tours Team • ⏱️ 5 min read • 📍 Red Sea Coast, Egypt • 📅 Scheduled Release: Tuesday, Dec 8, 2026

Planning your first marine excursion into the Red Sea? Knowing exactly what to bring on Hurghada boat trip adventures can make the difference between a sunburned, uncomfortable day and an unforgettable nautical journey. Whether you are sailing toward Orange Bay Island, swimming at Dolphin House, or embarking on a coral reef snorkeling excursion, this curated checklist covers all the essentials you must pack.

While modern luxury yachts provide snorkeling masks, fins, life vests, and freshly cooked buffet lunches, personal comfort and sun protection remain in your hands. Here is your definitive packing guide for cruising the Egyptian Red Sea.

1. Sun Protection & Skin Care Essentials

The Red Sea sun reflects strongly off open water and white yacht decks. Adequate protection is essential, even during cooler winter months from November to March:

☀️ Reef-Safe Sunscreen (SPF 50+)

Traditional chemical sunscreens damage delicate coral polyps. Always bring mineral-based, reef-safe sunscreen containing non-nano zinc oxide or titanium dioxide.

🕶️ Polarized Sunglasses with Strap

Polarized lenses cut harsh ocean glare, allowing you to spot coral formations beneath the water surface. A floating retention strap prevents them from falling into the sea.

👒 Wide-Brim Sun Hat or Cap

Choose a hat with a secure chin cord; afternoon breezes on the sundeck can quickly blow loose caps overboard during navigation.

👕 UV Protection Rashguard (SPF 50+)

A lightweight swimming shirt protects your back and shoulders from sun exposure while snorkeling for hours without washing off like sunscreen.

2. Dry Bags, Electronics & Underwater Gear

Water splashes, salt spray, and sandy island beaches require smart electronics management:

  • Waterproof Phone Pouch: A transparent lanyard case lets you take surface photos and videos while swimming without risking water damage.
  • Dry Bag (10L–15L): Keep your dry clothes, passport copies, wallets, and chargers safe inside a sealed dry bag while transferring onto zodiac speedboats.
  • Action Camera or Underwater Housing: The Red Sea's 30-meter visibility offers incredible underwater photography opportunities. Don't forget an extra battery and floating handle.
  • Power Bank: Taking hundreds of photos and videos depletes smartphone batteries rapidly under warm outdoor conditions.

💡 Pro-Tip: Hotel Towel Cards

Day-charter boats in Egypt rarely supply bath towels. Before leaving your resort in the morning, visit the towel hut with your hotel towel card and collect clean beach towels for every member of your party.

3. Comfort, Medications & Cash Tips

To ensure a smooth cruise from morning pickup until evening return:

  1. Seasickness Tablets: If you are prone to motion sickness, take dimenhydrinate or ginger tablets 30 to 45 minutes before the boat leaves the marina, not when you already feel dizzy.
  2. Light Windbreaker or Hoodie: Even in summer, the return cruise at 4:30 PM against sea breezes can feel breezy once your skin is damp from swimming.
  3. Small Cash Denominations (EGP / EUR / USD): Bring small cash for tipping the captain, dive guides, and kitchen crew, as well as souvenir photos taken by freelance onboard photographers.
  4. Slip-On Shoes / Flip-Flops: Shoes are removed upon boarding the yacht's wooden deck, but you will need footwear when walking onto hot island sands at Orange Bay or Paradise Island.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own towels on a Hurghada boat tour?
Yes. Most Red Sea day-cruises do not provide beach towels. You should borrow a towel card from your hotel reception the morning of your trip.
What currency is best for tipping crew on the boat?
Egyptian Pounds (EGP), Euros (EUR), or US Dollars (USD) in small denominations are widely accepted and greatly appreciated by local boat crews.
