Private Speedboat vs Yacht Charter in Hurghada: VIP Guide (2026)

✍️ Written by Capt. Tarek • ⏱️ 5 min read • 📍 Hurghada Marina & Islands • 📅 Scheduled: Thursday, Dec 4, 2026

For discerning travelers, couples celebrating anniversaries, or families seeking total privacy and personalized comfort, shared group boat tours can sometimes feel crowded. Booking a private yacht charter Hurghada or a private speedboat transforms your day on the Red Sea into an exclusive luxury adventure.

However, travelers frequently ask: Should we charter a high-speed private speedboat or a full-day luxury motor yacht? Both options offer immense advantages over shared group boats, but cater to distinctly different travel preferences, group sizes, and pacing.

Comparison: Speedboat vs Luxury Motor Yacht

Feature Private Speedboat Private Motor Yacht
Speed & Transit Time Very Fast (15–20 mins to Orange Bay) Relaxed Cruise (45–60 mins)
Pacing & Flexibility High agility; beats group crowds early Full day luxury lounge & sunbathing
Comfort & Facilities Open seating, bimini shade, swim ladder Sundeck, air-conditioned salon, restrooms, dining room
Dining Options Fresh fruit & soft drinks included Full open seafood / BBQ buffet prepared by chef
Ideal Group Size Couples & small families (1–6 guests) Families & friend groups (4–15 guests)
Starting Price From €120 (half-day) From €150 – €280 (full day private boat)

The Case for a Private Speedboat: Beat the Crowds

A private speedboat is the ultimate weapon for smart travelers who want to experience famous spots like Orange Bay Island or Dolphin House in total solitude. While large commercial cruise boats depart at 9:00 AM and arrive together at 10:30 AM, a private speedboat can depart at 7:30 AM and have you standing on the pristine sandbanks of Orange Bay before any other tourist sets foot on the island.

The Case for a Private Motor Yacht: Pure Floating Luxury

If your vision of a Red Sea holiday involves sunbathing on cushioned lounge mattresses, enjoying a freshly prepared seafood barbecue prepared by your private chef on board, and drifting leisurely between secluded reefs, a private motor yacht charter is unmatched.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a private yacht charter cost in Hurghada?
A full-day private luxury motor yacht charter for up to 10-15 guests starts from €150 to €300 depending on boat size, including private captain, fuel, crew, chef-prepared lunch buffet, and hotel transfers.
Can you customize the cruise itinerary on a private charter?
Yes! On a private charter you choose your own departure time and destinations. You can combine Orange Bay, wild dolphin swimming at Shaab El Erg, and remote reef snorkeling on your own schedule.
