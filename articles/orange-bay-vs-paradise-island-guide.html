Orange Bay vs Hula Hula Island: Which Hurghada Cruise is Best for You?

✍️ By Hurghada Sea Tours Editorial ⏱️ 4 min read 📍 Giftun Island, Hurghada 📅 Updated Nov 2026

🤖 Quick Answer & Key Takeaways (AEO Summary)

  • Orange Bay: Famous for iconic wooden water swings, wide shallow lagoon, and classic Maldives aesthetics.
  • Hula Hula Island: Hawaiian tropical boho beach club with bird-nest photo swings and relaxing vibes.
  • Cruise Duration: Both excursions spend 2 full hours on the island and include 2 offshore coral reef snorkeling stops.
  • All-Inclusive: Both include fresh chef-prepared buffet lunch, unlimited soft drinks, and hotel transfer.

Giftun Island is the undisputed jewel of Hurghada's coastline. Known as the 'Egyptian Caribbean', the island is home to several distinct beach resort setups, with Orange Bay and the newly trending Hula Hula Island leading the popularity charts.

If you only have time for one island boat cruise during your Hurghada vacation, which one should you choose? Here is our side-by-side local comparison.

Orange Bay: The Egyptian Maldives

Orange Bay is celebrated for its wide, shallow turquoise lagoon that gently deepens over hundreds of meters. Highlights include:

  • Iconic wooden overwater swings and hammocks right in the crystal-clear water.
  • Multi-level wooden boardwalks, beach bars, and beanbag seating.
  • The most recognized photography backdrop in the Red Sea.

Hula Hula Island: Boho Hawaiian Beach Club

Hula Hula Island brings an energetic Hawaiian-inspired aesthetic to Giftun Island. Highlights include:

  • Tropical boho styling with palm-thatched umbrellas and vibrant decor.
  • Dedicated photo zones including giant bird-nest swings and seaside swings.
  • Fun tropical beach music and relaxed island loungers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which island is better: Orange Bay or Hula Hula?
Orange Bay is famous for its Maldives-like overwater swings, iconic wooden pier, and extensive shallow waters. Hula Hula Island offers a boho-chic Hawaiian theme with unique photo swings, cozy beach lounges, and vibrant tropical decor.
How long do we stay on the island?
Standard full-day day cruises spend 2 full hours relaxing on the island beach, with the remainder of the day dedicated to 2 deep-sea snorkeling stops and lunch on board.
