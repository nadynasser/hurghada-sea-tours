Dolphin House Hurghada: Complete Guide to Swimming with Wild Dolphins (2026)

✍️ Written by Capt. Tarek & Editorial Team • ⏱️ 6 min read • 📍 Shaab El Erg Reef, Red Sea • 📅 Updated November 2026

Swimming alongside wild dolphins in their natural open-sea habitat is universally recognized as one of the most magical wildlife experiences on planet Earth. In Hurghada, Egypt, this dream is accessible every single day thanks to a world-famous offshore reef system known as Dolphin House (Shaab El Erg).

Unlike commercial marine parks or artificial dolphinariums where captive mammals perform scripted tricks, Dolphin House is a 100% natural, open marine sanctuary. Here, wild pods of Indo-Pacific bottlenose and spinner dolphins freely choose to rest, play, nurse their calves, and interact with snorkelers on their own terms.

What is Dolphin House (Shaab El Erg Reef)?

Geographically located approximately 25 kilometers north of Hurghada's marina, Shaab El Erg is a massive, crescent-shaped coral reef system. The reef's unique horseshoe formation creates a sheltered, calm lagoon protected from strong offshore winds and open ocean swells.

This protected lagoon acts as an ideal nursery and social hub for a resident pod of over 80 wild dolphins. The dolphins spend their nights hunting fish in deep offshore waters and migrate into the shallow, warm lagoon of Shaab El Erg during the morning hours to socialize, communicate, and rest.

🐬 Ethical Wild Encounter Guarantee

At Hurghada Sea Tours, our captains and certified divemasters adhere to strict international marine mammal conservation protocols. We never chase, corral, or touch wild dolphins. We position our yachts safely upcurrent and allow the dolphins to approach curious snorkelers voluntarily.

What to Expect on a Dolphin House Excursion

A typical full-day cruise to Dolphin House departs between 8:00 AM and 8:30 AM from the marina after you are picked up directly from your Hurghada hotel. The sailing journey to Shaab El Erg takes roughly 60 to 75 minutes on board our modern, two-deck cruise yachts.

Here is what an authentic day looks like:

  1. Morning Briefing & Gear Fitting: Our marine guides fit your sanitized mask, snorkel, and fins, while explaining dolphin behavioral cues and water safety.
  2. Dolphin Spotting & Snorkel Session: Upon reaching Shaab El Erg, the crew searches for active pods. When spotted, snorkelers enter the calm water in small groups accompanied by a guide. Watching mother dolphins swim synchronously with their young in 30-meter visibility is unforgettable.
  3. Second Snorkeling Session (Living Coral Gardens): After the dolphin encounter, the yacht moves to an outer reef plateau teeming with colorful clownfish, blue-spotted stingrays, sea turtles, and hard table corals.
  4. Fresh Open Buffet Lunch: Served fresh in the shaded dining salon, featuring grilled chicken, fish fillet, pasta, Egyptian rice, Mediterranean salads, and seasonal fruits.
  5. Water Sports & Scenic Cruise: Relax on the cushioned sundeck with complimentary soft drinks and water as the yacht cruises smoothly back to Hurghada by 4:00 PM.

Best Time of Year for Dolphin Sightings in Hurghada

Because Hurghada enjoys over 320 days of sunshine each year, Dolphin House trips operate 365 days a year. The wild pods inhabit Shaab El Erg reef year-round, meaning there is no "off-season" for dolphin encounters.

  • Spring & Autumn (April – June & September – November): Optimum sea temperatures (25°C to 28°C), gentle sea breezes, and ideal underwater visibility exceeding 30 meters.
  • Summer (July – August): Warmest waters (29°C), perfect for hours of effortless snorkeling without a wetsuit. Calves are frequently spotted during mid-summer.
  • Winter (December – March): Crisp blue skies and water visibility at its absolute clearest. Neoprene shorty wetsuits are provided on board to keep you warm and comfortable in 22°C to 23°C water.

Essential Tips for Your Dolphin House Trip

  • Do not touch the dolphins: Dolphin skin is delicate and sensitive to human bacteria and sunscreen chemicals. Let them dictate how close they wish to swim.
  • Avoid loud splashing: Calm, steady breaststroke kicks or gentle finning will attract inquisitive dolphins far more than frantic arm movements.
  • Use an action camera with a floating grip: Waterproof cameras like GoPros capture incredible 4K footage in the crystal-clear water. Always attach a buoyant floating strap so you don't lose your device in the deep reef sections.
  • Reef-safe sunscreen: Protect the delicate coral ecosystems by applying mineral-based zinc or titanium sunblock prior to boarding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is swimming with wild dolphins in Hurghada guaranteed?
Because Shaab El Erg is a natural open-sea habitat, wild dolphins are completely free. However, boat captains experience an approximately 90% sighting success rate year-round due to the pod using the horseshoe reef as a daily resting and socializing sanctuary.
Are the dolphins at Dolphin House in captivity?
No. At Hurghada Sea Tours, we strictly operate in the wild open Red Sea. The dolphins are 100% free, uncontained, and protected by Egyptian marine conservation laws. We strictly oppose artificial dolphinariums and captive shows.
How much does a Dolphin House tour in Hurghada cost?
A full-day Dolphin House cruise with Hurghada Sea Tours costs €25 per adult and €15 per child, which includes round-trip air-conditioned hotel pickup, all snorkel equipment, open buffet lunch, soft drinks, and two guided reef snorkeling sessions.
Can non-swimmers or children join the Dolphin House excursion?
Yes! Certified life jackets in adult and child sizes are provided free on board. Our professional snorkeling guides swim alongside beginners and families, providing flotation rings and guidance in the calm reef lagoons.
