FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know Before Sailing

How do I book a boat trip with Hurghada Sea Tours?
Booking is direct and seamless via WhatsApp. Simply pick your tour, tell us your preferred date, hotel name for pickup, and group size. We send you an instant written confirmation voucher. No credit card is needed upfront; you pay in cash (EUR, USD, GBP, or EGP) on the day of the trip.
Is hotel pickup and drop-off included?
Yes! Round-trip transfer in modern air-conditioned minivans is completely free of charge from all hotels in Hurghada city center and the Marina district. For hotels located in outer resorts (Makadi Bay, Sahl Hasheesh, El Gouna, or Soma Bay), we provide convenient transfer service for a minimal small extra fee (€5 per person).
Are snorkeling equipment and life jackets provided?
All essential equipment is included: sanitized masks, dry-top snorkels, fins in all shoe sizes, and buoyancy life jackets. If you or your children cannot swim, our certified rescue guides accompany you in the water with floating rescue rings so you can enjoy the coral reef with 100% peace of mind.
What should we bring with us on the trip?
Please bring your hotel beach towels, swimwear, sunglasses, a hat, sun cream, and a copy or photo of your passport. Cash is recommended for personal snacks or optional tips for the boat crew.
What is your weather and cancellation policy?
You can cancel or reschedule your reservation with zero penalty up to 24 hours before pickup. If the Egyptian Coast Guard restricts marine activity due to strong winds or rough seas, we reschedule your trip for the next calm day or cancel without any charges.
Is lunch on the boat suitable for vegetarians or allergies?
Yes, our daily onboard buffet features grilled fish/chicken alongside a rich variety of vegetarian dishes including rice, pasta, roasted potatoes, fresh Egyptian salads, and seasonal fruits. If you have specific dietary needs, just notify us when booking on WhatsApp.
